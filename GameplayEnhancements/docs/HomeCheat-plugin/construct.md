# construct (blueprints, feature Construction)

Built and tested 2026-10-25 on Valheim 1.0.20, BepInEx 5.4.2351, in the test world `ClaudeTest` on the host `MDENYSIUK-PERS`. Own game and own world only. Source: [src/Features/Construction/](../../src/Features/Construction/), DLL `HomeCheat.Cmd.construction.v<stamp>.dll`. Spell bar: `6.7.1 Construction...` (the panel), `6.7.2 Construction status`.

## Goal

Before: a blueprint downloaded for PlanBuild or InfinityHammer could not be used without those mods. After: the file goes into `BepInEx/config/homecheat/blueprints/`, the panel lists it, a preview shows where it will stand, and on confirm the ground is prepared and the construction is placed piece by piece as if built with the hammer, then reported: how many pieces stand, how well supported, what broke and why.

| reports | `step=status`, `step=support[&id=]`, `step=ground[&id=]`, `step=flatness`, `step=clear[&id=]`, `step=fit&name=<n>[keys as preview]`, `step=materials&name=` | read-only: plan and builds; support colours and the weak pieces by prefab, plus the last breakage; terrain under every 1 m cell; the last ground step against its targets; the world objects still in a build's clearing box (pivot or body, by kind, the first 8 named) and the build's own loose objects (the blueprint's bushes, pickables, animals); what `ground=fit` would raise at a spot, with and without the carried rule, against the terrain there now, and how many terrain zones another peer owns; the material families |

Keys: `name=<construction>` or `<folder>/<version>`; `at=aim` (default) `|self|x,z|x,y,z`; `yaw=` world degrees (default your facing, snapped to 22.5); `ground=auto` (default: on terrain, a rock, a tree or a bush `fit`, on a player-built piece `none`; a natural object under the crosshair is cleared before the build, so the base is the terrain under it) `|fit|flatten|pillars|none`; `base=floor` (default) `|lowest`; `material=blueprint` (default) `|<family>`; `overlap=1` builds over player-built pieces. Caps (refused): 4 000 pieces, 64 m a side, 6 000 levelled vertices, 20 copies, 50 listed; the foundation stops at 1 500 pieces.

## Files and settings

- Blueprints: `BepInEx/config/homecheat/blueprints/` (game folder, never this repository: other creators' content). A file = one construction; a folder = the versions of one construction.
- Formats read: PlanBuild (`#Name`, `#Creator`, `#Description`, `#Category`, `#SnapPoints`, `#Pieces`, a line `prefab;category;x;y;z;qx;qy;qz;qw;"text";sx;sy;sz`) and InfinityHammer (headers `#Center`, `#Coordinates`, `#Rotation`; a 14th field with the object's saved data in base64, counted, not applied). The text field goes to signs and to portal tags (`TextReceiver`).
- State: `BepInEx/config/homecheat/construct.state`, one plan per world. Every placed object carries the ZDO keys `hc_construct` (build id), `hc_construct_o`, `hc_construct_yaw`, `hc_construct_name`; the preview's columns, torches, doors and door poles `hc_construct_pillar` (the plan id).

## How it works

1. **Layout.** The footprint's centre goes to the anchor, turned by the yaw. The **ground floor** (the lowest 0.25 m level of pivots with at least 5 % of the pieces) sits with its collider bottom, measured on a hammer-style ghost (`PieceGhost`), on the base; pieces below it are foundations and sink.
2. **Ground** (`fit`). The old hoe stamps in the box are removed (they are `TerrainModifier` objects that return at the prefab radius after a reload), then one registered single-vertex `TerrainOp` per terrain vertex sets the target: the base, or the smooth surface (`FitField`) under pieces that need ground (no WearNTear, or a material other than wood), never above a piece. A non-wood piece that rests on non-wood with a foot of its own needs no ground (`FitField.Carried`, in the build's own frame: a stone plaza on stone walls stands on the walls; stone passes support only straight down, so a tile with no wall under it is a foot and still gets ground). The terrain zones another peer owns are taken over before every batch (the game drops an operation in a zone it does not own). One operation per vertex: nothing stacks, a repeat changes nothing.
3. **Check.** The terrain is measured against every target. More than 5 % off stops the build. Vertices more than 8 m from the world's own ground (the game's limit) get a **stone foundation**: 2 x 2 m `stone_floor_2x2` tiles turned with the build, `stone_wall_2x1` stacked under each down to the real ground.
4. **Clear the space.** Inside the pieces' box + 1 m, from 3 m under the lowest piece (the stone foundation on a slope included): trees, logs, rocks, stumps, bushes, pickables, saplings and items go when their pivot is in the box or their body reaches in (a solid collider, for a tree also its crown). Never characters, player-built pieces or own marks. The blueprint's own bushes and pickables are part of the build (`step=clear` lists them apart).
5. **Pieces** bottom-up in batches of 100 every 0.1 s, each with `Piece.SetCreator` and `WearNTear.OnPlaced` as `Player.PlacePiece` does. Support is reported at 5 s (settling) and 30 s (settled) with the broken pieces by prefab and reason (no ground under it, resting on pieces that gave way, on the ground).

6. **Preview doors** (`PreviewDoors`, 2026-10-25). The preview runs the same layout as the build and places every piece with a `Door` component except window shutters (at most 40) at its exact place and turn, without a creator or `OnPlaced`, so the keep-away check and the clearing ignore it. Its collider bottom (`PieceGhost.Box`) is raycast down at both ends of its width, 0.25 m in; a gap over 0.15 m gets `wood_pole_log_4` stacked from the bottom down to the surface (at most 10 a column). The rays skip objects of an older preview and objects destroyed in this frame (`PreviewDoors.SurfaceBelow`): a new preview or a rotate places its objects in the frame the old ones are taken down. 30 s later (unless the preview changed) the log reports how many doors stand and what each end rests on; `step=status` repeats that measurement; a fallen door shows on screen.

## What failed first

The lessons are folded into the `valheim-spells-craft` skill (`instructions/implement.md` and its evals). In short: the ground floor stood on its own foundation posts in the air; per-column terrain stamps made spikes; the hoe pieces' stamps shrank after a reload; own terrain operations were cancelled until registered in `ObjectDB.m_terrainOpsByHash`, and square ones stacked; the two gumdrop files are one house in two versions and stood inside each other; only stone needs ground; the 8 m terrain limit at a slope. Each was found by a measurement (`step=ground`, `step=flatness`, the breakage report, an alignment search), not by a guess.

## Change procedure

Code in `src/Features/Construction/`; `bash src/build.sh construction`; `op=reload`; test with the forms above, cheat state before and after. Unity-free parts (`BlueprintParse`, `BlueprintRepeat`, `FitField`) are tested in a scratch console build or in memory (PowerShell `Add-Type`; Smart App Control blocks new scratch exes): selftest covers only `Core/Pure`.

## Undo

`op=construct&step=undo&items=1&confirm=1` (the last build, or `id=`), or the hammer: the pieces are yours. The terrain is not restored. The preview (columns, torches, doors): `step=remove`.

## Open

- `Placeable_Stone` (a Pickable + Piece + WearNTear decor stone): about a third vanish, some on the ground; cause not found.
- The stone foundation breaks where the drop is about 9 m (5 of 111 pieces at the portal hub).
- Resume after leaving the world in the middle of a large build: not built (builds finish in seconds).
- A preview door or pole that breaks, or that someone takes down with the hammer, drops its materials (`WearNTear.Destroy` calls `Piece.DropResources`), as the marker poles did before the doors; `step=remove` and `step=start` destroy them without drops.
- After the 2026-10-25 re-level of the portal hub (`modestypooh-portalhub-20261025201715`) the game still shows its cached support (a terrain change does not clear it): whether every carried stone piece holds shows after the area reloads, or in a fresh build (`step=place`, then the settled line). One stone foundation piece near the 9 m drop broke after the first re-level (a `Stone` dropped at -484,-104).
- The build-time clearing with the body test and the deeper box is not run in game yet (only its read-only twin `step=clear`).
- `step=level` keeps a plan with `ground=none` and no preview: after the re-level the world `ClaudeTest` holds one for the hub (`step=status` shows it; `step=start` refuses it, the hub's own pieces are inside).
- World's `TerrainOps` (`groundlevel`, `path`) has no zone takeover: in a zone another peer owns, its operations are dropped too (architecture doc, Known defects).
- Families such as `loot`, `rug`, `scale` (furniture prefixes) are offered where a shape matches.
